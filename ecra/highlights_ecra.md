# Highlights

Estimating Online Demand with Sparse Geographic Data

- Spatial diagnostics detect strong spatial structure in only 17–42% of draws
- Plug-in Fay–Herriot intervals cover 88.5% and 87.6% of areas, against 95% nominal
- A simulated second wave identifies the variance split the intervals need
- On observed e-commerce counts, GeoAI beats Fay–Herriot in all 24 draws
- Estimator rankings do not transport from simulated to real surfaces
